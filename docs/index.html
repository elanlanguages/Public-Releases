  • XTRF for Windows

    Opens XTRF in its own window, apart from your browser tabs.

    v1.18.6Updated 20 November 2026

    Download 109 MB
    What’s new in 1.18.6
    • Register in XTRF: ELAN Chat's OCR converts up to three PDFs at the same time, each in its own discussion, so a long document no longer holds the others up.
    • Register in XTRF: the OCR step no longer keeps waiting once ELAN Chat is done: it takes the converted document when it has not changed for a minute and the agent has stopped working, even when ELAN Chat keeps its answer open. Each conversion is written to the log.

  • SwornFinder

    Find sworn translators and interpreters in Belgium, France, Germany and Poland.

    v2.6.0Updated 18 November 2026

    Download 57 MB
    What’s new in 2.6.0
    • New country: Poland (list of sworn translators of the Ministry of Justice, lista tłumaczy przysięgłych). It covers about 10,000 people and 57 languages, each combined with Polish both ways, for translation and interpreting, with the e-mail addresses (≈ 49%) and phones published by the list, a voivodeship filter and the TP number.
    • For each person, the date each language was granted, the TP number and the academic title appear in the tooltip and the export.
    • Better civility guess for Polish first names.
